# TeamSystem Product Catalog

## Reference  <!-- REQUIRED · source-silence: cite-only -->

This page inventories the **TeamSystem** commercial product portfolio and places it on the OnePlatform CityMap. It reconciles two normative lenses on the same portfolio: the **commercial offering** marketed by market vertical on teamsystem.com, and the **internal engineering register** (the adoption spreadsheet: Area → Functional Domain → module, each marked Legacy / Target / Business-Capability).[^1] It is a lookup surface — for the CityMap model itself see [[OnePlatform Architecture and CityMap]], and for how commercial products relate to business capabilities see [[Commercial Offer and Business Capability]] and the [[Business Capabilities Manifesto]]. Italian product names are proper nouns kept verbatim; canonical naming is governed by the [[OnePlatform Glossary]].

### CityMap placement[^1]

The catalog sits in the top two CityMap bands:

- **L8 — Commercial Offering.** The customer-facing suites and horizontals:[^1]
  - **TS in Cloud** (micro / SME suite) — Fatture in Cloud, Condomini in Cloud, Associazioni in Cloud, Commercialisti in Cloud, Cassa in Cloud, Sportivi in Cloud, TS Hospitality, Wellness in Cloud, Netlex in Cloud.
  - **TS Studio Cloud** (professionals) — TS Studio, TS Azienda, Studio HR, Studio Legal.
  - **TS Mid-Market Cloud** — TeamSystem Enterprise (TSE) + verticalised editions: TSE Construction, TSE Fashion, TSE Industry 4.0, TSE Wine & Oil, TSE Waste.
  - **Horizontals** — TS HR, TS Extended CRM, Trust Services, Cybersecurity, TS Digital Finance, Clicdata (BI/analytics).
- **L7 — Business Capabilities.** The capability families the products deliver:[^1] Finance, Tax, SCM, Manufacturing, HR services, Trust Services, Cybersecurity, Collaboration, FinTech, Data, Marketing, Compliance, eDocuments.

### Internal Area register (Legacy → Target)[^1]

The internal adoption register groups the portfolio into 15 engineering **Areas** — Architecture & Engineering, CAF, Construction, Data, Digital Finance, E-Procurement, Enterprise, Extended CRM, HRS, Legal, Micro ERP, Micro Vertical, Platform, Professionals, Trust & Cybersecurity — and marks each module **Legacy (L)**, **Target (T)**, or **Business Capability (BC)**.[^1] The Target line converges legacy products onto cloud editions: e.g. the LogicalSoft engineering tools (Termolog / Schedulog / Travilog, all L) map to the Architecture & Engineering (BC) targets; the TS STUDIO/TS AZIENDA and TS Via Libera suites (L) converge onto TS Studio Cloud / Professionals targets; the Alyante / Active / ACG ERPs (L) converge onto TSE V12 Cloud (T). The full per-module L/T/BC listing is in the cited source.[^1]

### Naming & confusables[^1]

Governed by the [[OnePlatform Glossary]]. Load-bearing distinctions:

- **The "…in Cloud" abbreviation family (FIC / DIC / CiC).** **Fatture in Cloud (FIC)** and **Dipendenti in Cloud (DIC)** are canonical; the abbreviations are kept verbatim and **not auto-expanded** blindly. **Condomini in Cloud** (internal "CiC") is a third, distinct product — do not conflate with **CiC Reviso** (a separate legacy micro-ERP).[^1]
- **TSE = TeamSystem Enterprise** — the mid-market ERP. "TeamSystem Enterprise Cloud" is its cloud brochure edition; "TSE V10/V12 Cloud" are the target engine versions.[^1]
- **Standalone product vs. embedded module.** **CRM in Cloud** (horizontal SaaS) is distinct from the **CRM** module inside TeamSystem Enterprise; **Cassa in Cloud** (POS, see [[Cassa in Cloud Tenancy and Franchising Use Cases]]) is distinct from **TeamSystem Hospitality** (hotel PMS).[^1]
- **Sector ERPs are products, not Enterprise modules.** Fashion / Wine / Oil / Agrifood / Construction / Waste are distinct verticalised products (and TSE editions), not modules within a single Enterprise install.[^1]
- **Condominium stack.** Domustudio (market-leading), Brainware (legacy heritage), and TeamSystem Condomini Fatture / CAF (invoicing / tax add-ons) are distinct offerings within the same vertical.[^1]
- **TeamSystem Waste vs. TeamSystem Waste 360 — NEVER-MERGE, TWO distinct products.** Waste is special-waste compliance (RENTRI/FIR/MUD; formerly named AIC) for waste producers/transporters/intermediaries/consultants; Waste 360 is ARERA-compliant integrated environmental-service management for the Utilities market. Maintainer decision (2026-07-23) supersedes the prior "Waste 360 = Waste, one product" ruling — the fresh 2026-07-23 per-product captures show two sibling pages at distinct URLs targeting distinct audiences.[^25][^26]
- **TeamSystem Enterprise Power-I vs. TeamSystem Enterprise / Enterprise Cloud.** Power-I is a SEPARATE product (distinct URL, no AI-feature set, RPG code base) — never merge into the Enterprise/Enterprise Cloud page.[^1]
- **TS Safety&Compliance vs. TS Sicurezza Cantieri.** Both are Construction-vertical site-safety products but cover non-overlapping scope: Safety&Compliance is digital site-badge/access/Idoneità-Tecnico-Professionale compliance; Sicurezza Cantieri is PSC/POS safety-plan drafting and risk assessment. Never merge.[^20][^21]
- **TS Asset Management vs. TeamSystem Asset Management (PA) — unresolved name collision.** The Construction-vertical `TS Asset Management` (Aziende/PA/Professionisti audience, fascicolo del fabbricato + AEC Explorer) and the PA-vertical `TeamSystem Asset Management (PA)` (municipalities, censimento/fascicoli) share a product name at two different URLs with non-overlapping feature descriptions; whether they are the same software or two SKUs is NOT established by either source — flagged, not merged.[^9][^17]
- **TS Finanza Agevolata vs. TeamSystem Finanza d'Impresa.** Both concern "finanza agevolata" (subsidized finance) but are distinct offerings: TS Finanza Agevolata is a direct-to-company advisory service; TeamSystem Finanza d'Impresa is a SaaS portal for professionals (commercialisti, consultants) managing Finanza Agevolata for clients. Never merge.[^1]
- **Coffee in Cloud / Caffè in Cloud — FICTIONAL, not a real product.** The ICE Products Catalog developer docs' own worked example (`code: COFFEE`, `label: Coffee In Cloud`) illustrating how to register a new product; never a marketed TeamSystem SKU and never a claimant of the real "in Cloud" suite (Fatture/Cassa/Contabilità/Commercialisti in Cloud, etc.).

### Coverage note[^1]

The Construction, Pubblica Amministrazione (PA), and Trust Services verticals above are each sourced from a curated per-product page (see the footnoted `source:webpage:ts-*` citations in "Portfolio by market vertical"), not from the aggregate catalog alone. Two residual gaps remain: **TeamSystem Cybersecurity** is sourced from the official "Aziende" (SME/enterprise) edition brochure — `teamsystem.com/store/ts-cybersecurity/` is a client-side-only SPA no fetch tool could render — and a parallel "Studi" (professional-studio) edition brochure has not been fetched.[^15] The **Contabilità in Cloud** and **Fatture in Cloud** store pages are likewise client-side-rendered and return navigation-only content; their detail is captured from the main product pages instead.[^1]

**2026-07-23 full-catalog gapfill (55 further products).** A full curl-verbatim re-capture of teamsystem.com produced 55 additional per-product source pages (`sources/webpage/ts-*.md`, bringing the curated set to 70 total), folded into every vertical above by citing `source:webpage:teamsystem-product-catalog-2026-07` (which itself now inline-cites each individual `source:webpage:ts-*` page in its Part A) — new products not previously named in this catalog got individual footnotes below, consistent with the prior Construction/PA/Trust convention; products already named in this catalog (e.g. TeamSystem Enterprise, Cassa in Cloud, Netlex in Cloud) simply gained a per-product source page and are cited via [^1]. This batch also (a) added the new **CAF** vertical, (b) split **TeamSystem Waste / Waste 360** into two products per maintainer decision, and (c) surfaced several previously-unlisted products (Enterprise Power-I, TS Asset Management, BPM Service, TS Engineering AI, TS Safety&Compliance, TS Sicurezza Cantieri, TeamSystem Cantieri, Be Amazing, CDA On Board, TS Digital Invoice, TS Finanza Agevolata, TeamSystem Finanza d'Impresa, TeamSystem Service Paghe, App Mobile TeamSystem HR AI) — see "Naming & confusables" above for the collisions this raised.

Treat the vertical index above as the marketed offering as of 2026-07, not an exhaustive SKU list.

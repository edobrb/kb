# OnePlatform Manifesto

At TeamSystem, we build a platform to support our services — to achieve economies of
scale and to ensure non-functional requirements. Our platform is the foundation of our
product strategy and supports all other products. Every design and service decision
must be aligned with our strategic objectives and respect the needs of the market we
serve.

Our approach is **"Platform as a Product"**, emphasizing that it is our first and
foremost product and serves all others in our portfolio. It must be developed with the
concentration, attention, and care that each product deserves.

We are committed to building a platform that follows these principles:

## 1. Modular and Interoperable

Modularity and interoperability allow new services to be integrated easily and existing
ones replaced without significant impact. The platform is designed with a modular,
agnostic architecture, independent of both products and underlying technologies. This
facilitates the integration and interchangeability of vendor-specific services: we can
upgrade or replace components without disruption, ensuring maximum flexibility and
supporting continuous innovation.

## 2. Consistent

The platform must be uniform and predictable, providing consistency across different
modules and services. We want to give developers, product teams, and end customers a
uniform, intuitive, and usable experience regardless of which part of the system they
are using — reducing the learning curve and improving operational efficiency.

## 3. Future-proof

We think long-term, ensuring the platform can evolve with new technologies and market
demands. The platform adopts modern but consolidated technologies to create solid,
sustainable technological foundations over the long term. We use Continuous Discovery
practices to anticipate market trends and user needs, ensuring the platform remains
relevant and creates value over time.

## 4. Sustainable

Sustainability — both technical and operational — is a central principle: to reduce
technical debt and maintain efficiency, lower costs, and lower consumption. We promote
green software-engineering practices to minimize environmental impact.

## 5. Self Service

Users must be able to configure and use services without depending on external
interventions — user autonomy is a key principle. Through detailed documentation and
self-service support, we reduce the need for direct technical assistance, improving
operational efficiency. We guide users with clear rules and best practices, maintaining
flexibility and context to foster innovation.

## 6. Resilient

The platform is designed to be robust, ensuring continuity even under stress or failure.
We ensure the system remains constantly available, stable, secure, and operational even
under stress — minimizing downtime, implementing failover mechanisms, and carrying out
regular maintenance and monitoring. The goal is to provide uninterrupted service and
maintain user trust by preventing failures and addressing issues quickly.

## 7. Cooperative

The platform must foster collaboration between teams, products, and users, creating an
integrated ecosystem. We believe in open collaboration, encouraging contributions,
integration, and knowledge sharing. The platform grows thanks to the contribution of
all those who use it: we expect and promote direct contributions, so that even answers
to specific needs can become standard through everyone's contribution and continuous
feedback.

## 8. Data sharing

Data is not just a by-product but a key value of the platform, designed to be accessible
and useful. We promote the certification and quality of data shared between services and
products. This guarantees reliable answers to users and exploits the opportunities the
market offers. The provenance and authenticity of content are priorities, to maintain
integrity and trust in information.

## 9. Flexible

The platform must adapt easily to different needs and variable contexts. We enhance the
elasticity of the platform's services through automation and rapid scalability, ensuring
both high performance and cost optimization. We monitor constantly to anticipate needs,
managing increasing volumes of data and transactions reliably.

## 10. Agnostic

The platform must not be tied to specific technologies or suppliers, offering maximum
flexibility. We design the system so that it does not depend on a specific technology,
supplier, or product.
